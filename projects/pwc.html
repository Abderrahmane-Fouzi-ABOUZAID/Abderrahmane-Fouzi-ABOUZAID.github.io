PwC x ACC / Consulting case / 3rd place

Digital energy transformation

A five-year roadmap for a Moroccan renewable-energy operator, designed to establish the governance and infrastructure required before scaling AI and connected operations.

Result
3rd place
Role
Solo case development
Sector
Solar, wind, and hydro
Horizon
Five-year roadmap

01 / Problem

AI ambition was ahead of the operating foundation.

The case described fragmented data, uneven technology adoption, and no clear governance for scaling artificial-intelligence use cases. Recommending isolated tools would not solve the underlying capability gap.

The proposed strategy therefore sequenced governance, data, connectivity, use cases, and adoption.

02 / Roadmap

Build the foundation before the intelligence layer.

GovernData ownership and cyber governance
ConnectIoT monitoring and edge processing
PredictMaintenance and production models
AdoptSkills and change management
MeasureUsage, reliability, and value KPIs
Foundation

Data Center of Excellence

Clarify data governance, accountability, standards, and cybersecurity ownership.

Operations

IoT and edge computing

Collect asset signals and process time-sensitive data closer to operations.

Use cases

Predictive maintenance

Prioritize practical models linked to equipment availability and production forecasting.

Adoption

Skills and KPIs

Pair technology deployment with workforce enablement and measurable usage.

03 / Outcome

A feasible strategy recognized for its depth.

The roadmap placed third in the PwC x ACC Consulting Case Game. Its central argument was that high-value AI depends on governed data, secure connected infrastructure, operational ownership, and adoption.
