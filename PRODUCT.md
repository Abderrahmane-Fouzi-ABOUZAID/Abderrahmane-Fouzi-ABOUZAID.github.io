# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Cybersecurity and software-engineering recruiters, hiring managers, technical leads, and professional peers reviewing Abderrahmane's experience, projects, and readiness for engineering opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Present Abderrahmane as a cybersecurity engineering student with balanced strengths in GRC and compliance, application security, and software development. The portfolio should make his Royal Air Maroc internship outcomes, technical depth, competition results, and ability to deliver reusable systems easy to evaluate and remember.

## Brand Personality

Technical, rigorous, and assured. The voice is concise and evidence-led, showing engineering judgment without exaggeration or generic cybersecurity theatrics.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon hacker aesthetics, anonymous terminal cosplay, decorative glassmorphism, repetitive card grids, inflated claims, buzzword-heavy copy, and portfolio templates that hide evidence behind visual effects.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the current professional identity and make the dual cybersecurity/software-engineering profile immediately clear.
2. Show concrete scope, architecture, safeguards, and outcomes instead of relying on broad skill claims.
3. Give the Royal Air Maroc work clear prominence while preserving breadth across engineering and competition projects.
4. Keep interactions purposeful, keyboard-accessible, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
5. Retain the dark technical identity while improving hierarchy, readability, responsiveness, and visual restraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for color contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, semantic structure, modal behavior, and reduced-motion support. Content must remain usable without animation and across mobile and desktop layouts.
